RULES, PRIZES & MVP HONOURS

Standard competitive Mobile Legends: Bang Bang guidelines enforced at Sekolah Rendah Kebangsaan Taman Gembira on 14/11/2026.

5V5 CUSTOM DRAFT PICK

All matches will be conducted in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Custom Mode: Tournament Draft Pick (5 Bans per team). All heroes released on the live server up to 7 days before the event are allowed.

Skin Effects: Allowed (Customary tournament skins or standard).

32 TEAMS KNOCKOUT

The tournament hosts strictly 32 registered squads in a single elimination championship tree: Round of 32 (16 matches), Round of 16 (8 matches), Quarter-Finals, Semi-Finals, and Grand Finals.

Match Length: Best of 1 (Rounds 1-3), Best of 3 (Semis & Finals).

PHYSICAL LAN STAGE

Held live on-site at Sekolah Rendah Kebangsaan Taman Gembira. Teams must check in physically with their registered squad members matching their verified MLBB Game IDs.

Spectators: Open to students, parents, and school supporters.

DEVICE & HARDWARE RULES

  • •
    Smartphones Only: iPads, tablets, emulators (BlueStacks/LDPlayer), and PCs are strictly banned.
  • •
    Power & Battery: Players must bring personal portable power banks and original charging cables.
  • •
    Audio: Wired or Bluetooth earphones/headphones are mandatory during matches to prevent sound spill.
  • •
    Network: Dedicated tournament Wi-Fi is provided, but players are advised to have 4G/5G mobile data backup.

FAIR PLAY & DISCIPLINARY CODE

  • •
    Anti-Cheat / Scripting: Any map-hacks, macro scripts, or modified APKs will result in immediate squad disqualification and ban.
  • •
    Roster Identity Match: Players must play strictly on the MLBB Game ID declared in their registration pass. "Joki" (account pilot) is strictly forbidden.
  • •
    Sportsmanship: Toxic chat, taunting, vulgarity, or unsportsmanlike behavior will incur penalty warnings or match forfeit.
  • •
    Referee Discretion: All in-game pause requests and match dispute determinations by official referees are final.
